About Malaysia

Malaysia is a country in Southeast Asia, with land on both the Malay Peninsula and the island of Borneo. Its capital is Kuala Lumpur, and Putrajaya is the federal administrative centre. Peninsular Malaysia shares a border with Thailand and a causeway and bridge with Singapore. East Malaysia, in Sabah and Sarawak, sits on Borneo beside Indonesia and Brunei.

The Geography of Malaysia

Malaysia covers about 330,000 km². Peninsular Malaysia sits south of Thailand, while Sabah and Sarawak lie across the South China Sea on Borneo. The land runs from highland tea country and rainforest to long island coastlines. Gunung Kinabalu in Sabah is the highest peak in the country.
